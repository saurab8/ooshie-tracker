Ooshie Tracker is built by TechTyra. This policy explains what data this tool collects and what it does not.

Your Collection Data

What you record, how many of each Ooshie you have, what you are willing to swap, and what you want, is stored only in your own browser, on your own device. It is never sent to, or seen by, TechTyra or anyone else, unless you choose to generate a swap QR code and show it to another person directly.

Swap QR Codes

When you generate a swap QR code, it is created entirely on your device and only contains your offer and want list. It is not uploaded anywhere. It only becomes visible to someone else if you physically show it to them to scan.

Visitor Counter

Ooshie Tracker keeps an approximate, anonymous count of browser profiles that have used the tool. A simple flag is stored only in your browser after you first change the tracker or generate a QR code, so that browser does not normally increase the count again. The shared server stores only the combined total. It does not store visitor identifiers, IP addresses, device details, collection data, or individual visit records. The count is not an exact number of people: using multiple devices or browsers, clearing browser storage, or using private browsing may count the same person more than once, while multiple people sharing one browser may be counted as one.

Contact Form

If you use the contact form, the name and email you enter are sent to TechTyra via a third-party form service so we can reply to you. This information is only used to respond to your message.

No Tracking, No Ads

This tool does not use advertising cookies, analytics trackers, or sell any data to third parties.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the contact form.
