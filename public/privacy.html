Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Ooshie Tracker is built by TechTyra. This policy explains what data this tool collects and what it doesn't.

Your collection data

What you record — how many of each Ooshie you have, what you're willing to swap, and what you want — is stored only in your own browser, on your own device. It is never sent to, or seen by, TechTyra or anyone else, unless you choose to generate a swap QR code and show it to another person directly.

Swap QR codes

When you generate a swap QR code, it is created entirely on your device and only contains your offer and want list. It is not uploaded anywhere. It only becomes visible to someone else if you physically show it to them to scan.

Visitor counter

The only information stored on a shared server is a count of unique visitors to this tool — a single number. No names, no device details, no collection data, and no way to identify who visited.

Contact form

If you use the contact form, the name, email, and message you enter are sent to TechTyra via a third-party form service (Web3Forms) so we can reply to you. This information is only used to respond to your message.

No tracking, no ads

This tool does not use advertising cookies, analytics trackers, or sell any data to third parties.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the contact form.
